# Arena-Hard & LLM-as-a-Judge Calibration (Li et al., LMSYS 2024)

## 1. Bradley-Terry Preference Modeling
Automated model benchmarking via pairwise comparisons relies on the **Bradley-Terry (BT) model**:
$$P(i \succ j) = \sigma(r_i - r_j) = \frac{e^{r_i}}{e^{r_i} + e^{r_j}}$$
where $r_i$ represents the latent skill rating of model $i$. Parameters are solved via Maximum Likelihood Estimation across pairwise comparison matrices.

## 2. Systematic Judge Biases & Mathematical Mitigations
Lianmin Zheng et al. (NeurIPS 2023) and Tianle Li et al. (arXiv:2406.11939) formalize four core LLM-as-a-Judge biases:
1. **Position Bias:** Judges disproportionately favor candidate response 1 or 2 ($15\%\text{--}25\%$ win swings).
   - *Mitigation:* Bidirectional swap symmetrization:
     $$S(A, B) = \frac{1}{2}\left[\mathbb{I}(\text{Judge}(A, B) = A) + \mathbb{I}(\text{Judge}(B, A) = A)\right]$$
2. **Verbosity Bias:** Superficial preference for longer, verbose Markdown responses regardless of accuracy.
   - *Mitigation:* Length-penalized Bradley-Terry regression:
     $$P(i \succ j) = \sigma\left((r_i - r_j) + \beta(\text{len}_i - \text{len}_j)\right)$$
3. **Self-Enhancement Bias:** Models favor completions generated by their own model family.
   - *Mitigation:* Mixture of Judges (MoJ) combining independent model providers (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Gemini 1.5 Pro).

## 3. The Arena-Hard Pipeline
Arena-Hard-Auto extracts 500 challenging prompts from 200,000+ real-world Chatbot Arena conversations via the BenchBuilder pipeline:
- Compares models against a single fixed anchor (`gpt-4-0314`), reducing tournament complexity from $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$ to $\mathcal{O}(N)$.
- Achieves **$89\%\text{--}98\%$ correlation** with crowdsourced human Arena Elo ratings at $\sim \$25$ evaluation cost per model.
